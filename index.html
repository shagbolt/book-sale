How to use

Ringing up a customer

  • Tap + once for each item, or tap the number and type a count.
  • Deals are applied automatically. Watch for the red note at the bottom: it tells you when one more item would be free or cheaper, so you can let the customer know.
  • For anything with a price sticker, type the price under Priced as marked and tap Add. Tap × to remove one.

Reading the receipt

The receipt lists each kind of item, any deal or ½ price savings, and the total. On an iPad turned sideways it sits on the right; otherwise scroll down to see it.

Finishing a sale

  • Next customer saves the sale and clears the screen.
  • Clear starts over without saving anything.

Emailing a receipt

  • Tap Email this receipt under the receipt. Your mail app opens with the receipt filled in; type the customer's email address and send.
  • For an earlier sale, tap Email next to it in Today's receipts.
  • With no Wi‑Fi, the email waits in your mail app's Outbox and sends once you're back online.

Customer comes back for more

  • Scroll to Today's receipts and tap Reopen on their sale.
  • Add the extra items, then tap Save sale #. The receipt shows how much more to collect.
  • Cancel closes the sale without changing it.

½ price day

Tap ½ price day near the top. Everything, including deals and marked prices, is half off, rounded up to the nearest nickel. It stays on until you tap it again.

End of the day

  • Tap Export day summary and Export all sales and send the files to the treasurer.
  • Then tap Start a new day twice to clear the day's sales.
  • Each phone or iPad keeps its own records, so export from every device.

Good to know

  • Once the app has been opened online, it works without Wi‑Fi or cell data.
  • To change prices, deals or kinds of items, tap Prices at the top.

Price settings

Items in the same price group share a deal. Leave the deal blank for no deal. Changes apply to this device only.
